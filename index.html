RV VYAPAR SETU

Build Your Trading Business With Our & Associated Brands

A comprehensive trading empowerment initiative. Whether you are an existing distributor or a rising entrepreneur, RV Vyapar Setu provides ready-to-sell inventory, credit terms, regional exclusivity, and direct group pricing.

For Plant & Machine Owners

RV REVERSE TRADE

Turn Manufacturing Into Opportunity for Our Brands

Do you possess idle machinery or manufacturing capacity? RV Reverse Trade connects your factory infrastructure into HRRK's verified production network with structured buyback and long-term supply contracts.

Capacity Monetization: Fill idle machine hours with contract manufacturing of group branded items.
Raw Material & Mould Linkages: We supply specialized tooling, technical guidance, and polymer sourcing assistance.
Guaranteed Buyback Offtake: Pre-agreed off-take purchase orders with clear payment schedules.
