Founder’s Message

HRISHIKESH KADAM

Founder & Visionary — HRRK Group of Companies

Building Businesses. Creating Opportunities. Growing With Purpose.

Hrishikesh Kadam is the Founder and driving force behind HRRK Group of Companies, a diversified business ecosystem built around manufacturing, business opportunities, consumer brands and global trade.

With a vision to build businesses that create value across the entire journey—from manufacturing and product development to trading, brands and international markets—he is developing HRRK as a connected ecosystem of businesses rather than a single-industry company.

Under his leadership, the group is developing multiple business verticals including Namrushii Industrixa for manufacturing, private label and OEM operations; Rekhraaj Ventures for structured business opportunities; Rekhraaj Vyom for consumer brands; and a dedicated global exports division connecting Indian products and manufacturing capabilities with international markets.

His approach focuses on three key principles

BUILD

Build strong businesses, products and capabilities.

PARTNER

Create opportunities for entrepreneurs, traders, manufacturers and businesses.

GROW

Develop sustainable businesses and brands with a long-term vision.

His Vision

“To build a diversified, scalable and trusted business group that creates products, opportunities and businesses capable of growing from local markets to global markets.”

Today, Hrishikesh Kadam continues to work toward building an ecosystem where manufacturing creates products, businesses create opportunities, brands create value, and global trade creates new possibilities.

BUILD. MANUFACTURE. TRADE. GROW.

The vision behind HRRK Group of Companies.
