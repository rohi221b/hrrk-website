RV REVERSE TRADE™

A Structured Manufacturing Partnership & Buyback Ecosystem

RV Reverse Trade™ by Rekhraaj Venturis enables entrepreneurs, individuals, SHGs and small manufacturing partners to establish standardized micro-manufacturing units with machinery, raw materials, packaging, training, quality support and an organized buyback system.

Rolling production: QC, buyback and settlement under one framework.
Company-controlled brands: Partners can produce for Nivashaa, Scribexa, Devyana, Gloy and other approved SKUs.
Formal agreement: Machinery, materials, training, QC and buyback sit under a Manufacturing Support Agreement. This is not employment and does not guarantee income.

Manufacturing Support Agreement

Your Formal Entry into RV Reverse Trade™

Every participating manufacturing partner operates under a Manufacturing Support Agreement with Rekhraaj Venturis covering machinery assistance, raw-material supply, technical support, quality procedures, buyback participation and settlement.

The agreement is not an employment agreement and does not represent a guaranteed income or guaranteed return. Buyback applies only to approved quantities that meet quality, packing, documentation and program rules.

01. Machinery SupportInstallation, operating guidance, maintenance and repair assistance as per policy.
02. Manufacturing TrainingProcess, machine operation, batch preparation, packing and basic quality practices.
03. Raw Material SupportApproved materials, packaging, components, labels and batch-wise supply.
04. Technical SupportPhone, WhatsApp, video assistance, troubleshooting and process updates.
05. Quality SupportSpecifications, SOP, batch inspection and approved/rejected quantity recording.
06. Buyback SupportApproved finished goods enter settlement subject to quality, quantity and agreement validity.
